Hello! I am Kaicheng Li, an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, with a focus on artificial intelligence. I earned my B.S. in Mathematics, following the Applied Mathematics track, from The Ohio State University in 2024, with a minor in Computer Information Systems. I currently work as a research intern in the Laboratory of Machine Learning, Health and Biomedicine at USC, provide research support at Florida State University’s AIMHealth Innovation Lab, and serve as a research assistant in the Responsible AI Lab at the University of North Texas.

My research interests center on natural language processing for mental health counseling: understanding client resistance, developing realistic client simulators, and evaluating whether conversational models behave in ways that align with human expectations.

Selected projects

Detecting Incomplete and Evasive Answers with Request-Level NLP

2026

Collaborated on an NLP pipeline that decomposes financial earnings-call questions into individual requests, classifies answer coverage, and aggregates predictions into three response categories. Built 16,629 question–answer pairs and compared five classical classifier families with full-parameter fine-tuning of Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B.

69.1% macro-F1 · 71.5% accuracy · 597-example held-out reference set

The request-level Qwen3-1.7B approach improved macro-F1 by 5.0 percentage points over the same model’s whole-question baseline.

QianTick: Multimodal Deep Learning System for Short-Term Stock Prediction

2026

Constructed a temporal convolutional network in PyTorch for binary classification of stock price movements within a multimodal deep learning project. Conducted a literature review and comparative testing of candidate models.

Evaluating Model Robustness to Distribution Shift via SAM

2026

Developed a PyTorch pipeline to train ResNet-18 on CIFAR-10, comparing Sharpness-Aware Minimization with momentum-based SGD. Conducted validation-based hyperparameter tuning and optimized training workflows.
